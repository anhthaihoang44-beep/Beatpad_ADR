# BeatPad — Licenses and Audio Attributions

## 1. Legal Guardrail & Originality Notice
BeatPad is an original beat-making application inspired by the launchpad music creation genre. It does NOT use any trademarked assets, logos, sound packs, or copyrighted material from Easybrain or Drum Pad Machine. The name **BeatPad** is defined centrally in `AppConfig.APP_NAME`.

## 2. Built-in Audio Samples & Sound Packs
All 98 audio samples included in BeatPad across all 8 genre sound packs and metronome cues were generated programmatically via digital signal processing (DSP) synthesis algorithms using `tools/AudioSynthesizer.java`.
